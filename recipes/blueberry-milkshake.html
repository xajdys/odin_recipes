Blueberry Milkshake

Description

A thick and creamy milkshake blended with fresh blueberries, vanilla ice cream, and milk. Simple, refreshing, and delicious!

Ingredients

  • 2 cups vanilla ice cream
  • 1 cup milk
  • ½ cups blueberries
  • 1 banana
  • ¼ cup white sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Directions

  1. Place vanilla ice cream and milk in a blender; blend until smooth.
  2. Add blueberries, banana, sugar, and vanilla extract; blend until evenly mixed.
